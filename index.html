Test Outfit-Mesh-Linker Weight Transfer Preview

Attach mesh-only or rigged GLB outfits to a VRM humanoid skeleton — local browser processing.

1. Import models

For accurate transfer, export Body and Outfit in the same scale, coordinate space, and rest pose. Files stay on your device; no model upload.

2. Preview

Import a VRM Body and GLB Outfit.

Drag to rotate · Pinch or scroll to zoom. Transferred Outfit meshes are bound to the Body skeleton for preview.

3. Skeleton linking

Waiting for a VRM Body and Outfit.

Surface transfer finds the closest Body triangle and interpolates its skin influences using barycentric coordinates. For performance on mobile, the source surface is sampled and the transfer runs in chunks.

4. Export & compatibility

VRM export is not enabled yet. This build can load VRM 0.x / 1.0 and perform a real in-memory weight transfer, but it does not yet serialize the result back into a standards-compliant VRM file.

No UniVRM is used. The exporter must preserve the original VRM version, humanoid mapping, expressions, materials, spring bones / secondary nodes, colliders, and valid glTF skin data. A plain GLB export must not be mislabeled as VRM.
